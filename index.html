LocalOptima analytics

A private tool for the Local Optima YouTube channel.

What it does

It reads the channel's own statistics from YouTube: views, watch time, subscribers, likes, comments, shares, traffic sources, audience retention, impressions and click-through. It saves them as files for the channel's owner, so each video's first-day, first-week and first-month numbers are kept without opening YouTube Studio.

It only reads. It can't upload, edit or delete anything, and it can't post or reply to comments.

Who it's for

Only the Local Optima channel's owner uses it, signed in with the channel's own Google account. It isn't a public service, and there's nothing here for anyone else to sign up for.
